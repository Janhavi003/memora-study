# Product Specification - Phase 1

Phase 1 delivers a frontend prototype using mock services. Metrics are identified as demo data. Real persistence and production algorithms are deferred.

## Workflows
- Explore landing page/demo.
- Browse, search, filter, create, rename, duplicate, and delete decks.
- Paste/import text and review editable card candidates.
- Study one card at a time, reveal answers, and self-assess.
- Complete a multiple-choice quiz.
- Inspect review queue, statistics, and settings.

## UX
Warm editorial identity, responsive layout, accessible labels, focus states, keyboard study controls, and useful action feedback.
